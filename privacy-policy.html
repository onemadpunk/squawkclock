Squawk Clock privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Squawk Clock is an alarm clock and sleep-sound app. The short version: it has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't run any servers that collect information about you.

What stays on your phone

Your alarms, settings, chosen sounds, timer, and the town you pick for the weather are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. If you uninstall the app, they go with it.

Weather and location

The weather features are part of the optional unlock. To show a forecast, the app sends the latitude and longitude of the town you chose (or, if you choose "use my location", of your phone's rough location) to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute's free weather service, api.met.no, which returns the forecast. No name, account or device identifier is sent. Location permission is optional and only used for this. Town search uses a list of places stored inside the app, so typing a town name sends nothing anywhere. If you choose "use my location", your phone's own location services may be used to find the name of the nearest town, and that is handled by Android, not by us.

Purchases

The one-off unlock is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We only learn, through Google Play, whether this Google account has bought the unlock, and we receive no payment details or personal information.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Notifications: to show the alarm screen and the bedtime reminder.
  • Exact alarms and full-screen alerts: so alarms ring on the minute, over the lock screen.
  • Battery optimisation exemption: so your phone doesn't stop the alarm overnight.
  • Vibration, keep awake, and foreground service: to ring and vibrate reliably and to play sleep sounds.
  • Start-up: to put your alarms back after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: only to fetch the weather forecast and to talk to Google Play for the purchase.
  • Approximate location (optional): only for local weather, as above.
  • Camera flash: only for the torch button.

Children

Squawk Clock isn't aimed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
